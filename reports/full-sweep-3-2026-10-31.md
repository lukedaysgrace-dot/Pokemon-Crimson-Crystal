# Crimson Crystal: fixes + third full sweep (2026-10-31)

Proton's dialogue is untouched.

## What was fixed

### Egg groups (now official)
| Species | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Bagon, Shelgon, Salamence | Field | Dragon |
| Ralts, Kirlia, Gardevoir, Gallade | Field | Human-Like + Amorphous |
| Venipede, Whirlipede, Scolipede | Field | Bug |
| Feebas, Milotic | Water 1 + Water 2 | Water 1 + Dragon |
| Anorith, Armaldo | Water 3 + Bug | Water 3 |
| Sylveon | Field + Fairy | Field |

**Kept on purpose:** Lileep/Cradily stay Water 3 + **Plant**. Your
`tools/testdata/authored_move_restorations.json` asks for STUN SPORE to be a
Lileep egg move, and only Plant-group fathers can pass it on. Making them Water 3
only (official) would make that move impossible to get. Say the word if you'd
rather go official and move STUN SPORE to a tutor instead.

### Growth rates (where the engine has the official curve)
- Slow: Bagon, Shelgon, Salamence, Ralts, Kirlia, Gardevoir, Gallade
- Fast: Duskull, Dusclops, Dusknoir
- Medium Fast: Duraludon, Archaludon, Tyrunt, Tyrantrum, Amaura, Aurorus, Noibat, Noivern, Salandit, Salazzle
- Medium Slow: Cetoddle, Cetitan, Flittle, Espathra

Note: any of these already in a save file will have EXP that doesn't line up with
the new curve until they level up again.

### Egg hatch times (step cycles) for 42 species now official
Marill, Azumarill, Bagon line, Electivire, Glaceon, Leafeon, Magmortar, Mismagius,
Venipede, Whirlipede, Togekiss, Anorith, Charcadet line, Duraludon, Archaludon,
Shroomish, Breloom, Grubbin line, Croagunk, Drifloon, Drifblim, Fletchling line,
Lotad line, Scraggy, Scrafty, Kleavor, Glimmet, Glimmora, Noibat, Noivern,
Finizen, Palafin. Most were the template default of 20.

### Other fixes
- **"Original" stats mode:** Jirachi and Phantump were missing from
  `OriginalPokemonStats`, so they used the buffed stats even in Original mode.
  Added with official stats.
- **BRIGHTPOWDER** can now be bought at Mahogany's held-items counter (¥2000).
  Its price was 10.
- **Fan Club chairman bug** fixed: if your bag is full he now remembers you heard
  the speech.

### Test tools brought up to date
- `audit_moves.py` now scans every `animations*.asm` file and counts the new
  HeldItemTrigger animation.
- Stale stored hashes updated after your own later changes: the `main.asm` hash,
  the evolution hash (after your Flygon 45→40 change) and the battle-animation
  support hashes.
- `audit_resources.py` skips the not-yet-hooked-up Shucks tileset file.
