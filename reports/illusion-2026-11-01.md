# Illusion implementation and verification

Completed November 2, 2026 (America/Chicago).

Totodile's first ability slot is temporarily **Illusion** for testing. Torrent
and Sheer Force remain in slots 2 and 3. The new ability is appended to the
ability table; saved personality slots and the save format are unchanged.

## Behavior

- On each entry, disguise as the last conscious, non-Egg party member, unless
  that member is the entering Pokemon itself. Wild Pokemon without a party
  cannot create a disguise.
- Copy the apparent species, nickname, gender, shiny palette, cries, and battle
  graphics, including Unown's DV-selected appearance. Keep the holder's actual
  species, typing, level, HP, stats, moves, ability and held item.
- A direct damaging hit reveals the real Pokemon and grants **one Speed
  stage** if it survives. The active disguise is cleared before the reveal and
  boost, so later hits and subsequent hits of a multi-hit move cannot repeat it.
- Switching out resets the normal stat stages. Switching back in creates a new
  eligible disguise and allows another reward when that new disguise breaks.
- Misses, Protect, ordinary Substitute damage, poison, weather, Leech Seed,
  recoil and entry hazards do not break Illusion. Sound moves and Infiltrator
  reveal it when they damage the actual holder through Substitute.
- Mold Breaker does not bypass Illusion. Trace cannot copy it, Skill Swap
  fails, and Transform/Imposter fail if either battler is actively disguised.
  A revealed holder can be copied by Transform; that does not start a disguise.
- Neutralizing Gas prevents or ends a disguise. Gas ending does not restore it.
  Suppression and leaving the field do not grant the custom
  direct-hit Speed reward. Haze can remove the Speed stage but cannot restore
  an already broken disguise.

The standard entry, damage, and transfer behavior was checked against
[Pokemon Showdown's Illusion implementation](https://github.com/smogon/pokemon-showdown/blob/master/data/abilities.ts)
and [Transform rules](https://github.com/smogon/pokemon-showdown/blob/master/sim/pokemon.ts).
The Speed reward is this game's requested custom behavior. The game currently
implements Skill Swap, Trace, Transform, Imposter, Mold Breaker and Neutralizing
Gas; it has no Role Play, Entrainment, Gastro Acid, Worry Seed, Mummy or Receiver.

## Validation

- RGBDS 0.5.2 builds of both `pokecrystal.gbc` and `pokecrystal_debug.gbc`: pass.
- `make audit-static`: pass, including game data, text widths, move/learnset,
  trainer, save, linked resources, icons and map/trainer sprite checks.
- `96-illusion.yaml`: **29 passed**. Includes the actual 1.5x Speed value, repeat
  hits, native switches, both battle sides, hazards, status/residual damage,
  Substitute bypass, fixed damage, multi-hit moves, Future Sight, Haze, Speed
  cap, Transform in both directions, Trace, Skill Swap and Gas transitions.
- Every one of the game's **443 moves** executes against a legally disguised
  Totodile in the generated move coverage.
- Every **172 supported opposing abilities** tested against legal active
  Illusion: **172 passed**. The tests assert the pre-hit disguise state and the
  exact final Speed stage, including suppression by Gas.
- Full existing battle tests, all-move/effect/ability sweeps, Illusion move
  coverage and general ability execution/suppression/bypass/transfer matrices:
  **16,118 passed**. The final user-side Transform restriction was then checked
  against all **172 Transform/ability combinations**, all passing. A full main
  regression rerun on the final ROM, including all 29 focused cases and the 172
  active opposing-ability cases, passed **4,321 battles with no failures/errors**.
- Native graphical comparisons: exact front/back tile data and palettes match
  ordinary Gengar before reveal and ordinary Totodile after reveal. Also pass
  shiny/HUD-star and gender checks on both sides for Magnemite, Nidoran F,
  Tauros, Mienshao (internal species 500), and Unown. The visual fixture installs
  NPC lead DVs before entry so the Unown control uses the requested letter;
  ordinary debug overrides occur after entry.
- The 65.08-second video records native damage, stat changes and switching with
  game audio. MP4 video/audio decoding passes. No player save files are opened.

## Additional historical audits

All remaining `tools/audit_*.py` scripts were also run in check mode. Eight
passed. Three have separate archival/reference limitations:

1. `audit_attacking_learnsets.py` freezes historical runtime hashes. Its first
   failure is `main.asm`, whose receipt matches the original HEAD file and
   deliberately fails after the authorized new Illusion section is added.
   Its stored receipt was not changed.
2. `audit_mae_animations.py` reports a stale dependency hash for
   `constants/battle_anim_constants.asm`. That file was untouched by this task;
   the manifest also disagrees with the original HEAD contents.
3. `audit_sour_apple_completeness.py` requires a local upstream checkout which
   is missing `data/moves/special_animations.asm` in this environment.

These findings are separate from the passing current static and native battle
checks. No learnsets, existing dialogue, historical receipts or unrelated
animation sources were changed to silence them. Existing Weavile/source-art
edits were present before this task; the normal build regenerated its derived
graphics.
