# Crimson Crystal — second full sweep (2026-10-31)

Proton's two lines are restored exactly as written ("Damn it! Not to" in
`maps/RadioTower4F.asm`, "stopped a damn" in `maps/SlowpokeWellB1F.asm`).

This sweep built the ROM from your current source (rgbds 0.5.2, no warnings) and
ran all 22 of the repo's `tools/audit_*.py` scripts plus new checks covering
text, maps, events, Pokémon data, items, wild data and graphics. Nothing below has
been changed except the test hash noted in §4.

---

## 1. Pokémon data that doesn't match the official games

These are the only real data problems found. They change breeding compatibility
and EXP curves, so they're worth deciding on.

### Egg groups (look like template leftovers: most are `EGG_GROUND`)
| Species | In game | Official |
|---|---|---|
| Bagon, Shelgon, Salamence | Field | **Dragon** |
| Ralts, Kirlia, Gardevoir, Gallade | Field | **Human-Like + Amorphous** |
| Venipede, Whirlipede, Scolipede | Field | **Bug** |
| Feebas, Milotic | Water 1 + Water 2 | Water 1 + **Dragon** |
| Lileep, Cradily | Water 3 + Plant | Water 3 only |
| Anorith, Armaldo | Water 3 + Bug | Water 3 only |
| Sylveon | Field + Fairy | Field only |

### Growth rates
The engine has no Erratic/Fluctuating curves, so Swablu, Altaria, Shroomish,
Breloom, Drifloon, Drifblim, Lileep, Anorith, Cranidos, Shieldon, Applin,
Feebas, Zangoose and Seviper (and their evolutions) were approximated. That's
expected. These ones *do* have a matching curve in the engine but use a different one:

| Species | In game | Official |
|---|---|---|
| Bagon line, Ralts line (incl. Gallade) | Medium Slow | Slow |
| Duskull, Dusclops, Dusknoir | Slow | **Fast** |
| Dipplin | Medium Slow | Slow |
| Duraludon, Archaludon | Medium Slow | Medium Fast |
| Tyrunt, Tyrantrum, Amaura, Aurorus | Medium Slow | Medium Fast |
| Noibat, Noivern, Salandit, Salazzle | Medium Slow | Medium Fast |
| Cetoddle, Cetitan | Medium Fast | Medium Slow |
| Flittle, Espathra | Medium Fast | Medium Slow |

If any of these were balance choices, ignore them.

## 2. Items
- **BRIGHTPOWDER has no way to get it.** It isn't in any mart, item ball, hidden
  item, gift, prize list or wild held item; only Battle Tower trainers hold it.
- Key Items pocket: there are 28 key items for 25 slots. In normal play you hold
  about 22 at most (several get used up or handed over), so it's fine today. Keep
  it in mind if you add more.

## 4. Repo test tools that are out of date
These fail even on the source as it was before my edits, so the tools are what
need updating:
- `audit_moves.py` reports 38 "pointer has no script" errors because it doesn't
  scan `data/moves/animations7.asm`, where those animations live.
- `audit_attacking_learnsets.py`: its stored hash of `main.asm` is out of date.
  The same file had a stored hash of `engine/events/move_deleter.asm` that my
  Move Reminder text fix broke; I updated it in
  `tools/testdata/attacking_learnset_review.json` and
  `tools/testdata/historical_move_access.json`.
- `audit_mae_animations.py`: its stored hash of `constants/battle_anim_constants.asm` is out of date.
- `audit_resources.py` expects `pokecrystal_debug.gbc` and the unbuilt Shucks tileset.
- `audit_sour_apple_completeness.py` needs its `.tmpbuild` folder generated first.
