# Crimson Crystal — no-item tier list

Completed October 27, 2026 using the pinned October 24 game build. Newer game-source changes are outside this audit; see [resume provenance](RESUMED-2026-10-27.md).
Internal development report — October 24, 2026.

**231 final-stage/single-stage species/forms; 664 legal species/ability variants; 6621 screened build/strategy variants; 148,562 native-ROM battles.**

Both sides use **NO_ITEM**. Level 100, perfect DVs, zero stat experience, fresh HP/status, native entry effects and weather. Starter families and all unevolved/middle stages are excluded; legendaries are included.

## Method

1. Each species gets a shared portfolio of legal coverage, physical, special, recovery, status, priority, weight, setup, ability-aware and alternative movesets. Every legal ability is tested with the same portfolio. Setup/status openings are performed as real battle turns; no free boosts or pre-status are injected.
2. Screen against eight varied opponents, alternating player/enemy positions. Advance two genuinely different movesets **per ability**, wherever the legal pool permits.
3. Validate these on sixteen different opponents, playing **both sides** for 32 games per build. All abilities retain representation regardless of screening rank. The ability winners CSV contains this pass.
4. Select each species' best validated build before the final league. Every selected build then fights every other selected species twice with sides swapped: **460 games per Pokémon**. The tier list uses only this league.

Move legality and ability slots come from this game's source; damage, move effects, weights and ability effects execute in the compiled game through PyBoy. Native logs and scenario seeds are retained in the audit database.

## Limits

This is a measured singles benchmark under automated move selection, not an exhaustive solution of every legal moveset, perfect play, or a team-building tier list. The final league evaluates one selected build per species. Ability differences can depend on the moveset and opponent. Ties share rank; alphabetical order within ties has no strength meaning. Turn limits are unresolved and contribute no wins; score_upper includes their possible points. S/A/B/C/D/E thresholds are 87.5/75/62.5/50/37.5/25%; F is below 25%.
