# Attacking learnset review

498 roster entries reviewed. 179 have edits: 356 attacks/setup options moved out of natural progression; 13 delayed moves.

All original level-1 moves, evolution methods and evolution rewards are unchanged. Viable mixed attackers retain both categories. Early moves and useful priority, pivoting, debuffs, fixed damage, Body Press and ability interactions are preserved. Optional acquisition routes remain available.

These are level-up changes. TM/HM compatibility and family egg lists are retained.
