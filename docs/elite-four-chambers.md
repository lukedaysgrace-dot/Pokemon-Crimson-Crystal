# Elite Four chambers

The five League rooms each have their own tileset, palettes, collision and
ambient animation. The gyms and shops that share the old
`TILESET_ELITE_FOUR_ROOM` / `TILESET_CHAMPIONS_ROOM` are untouched.

| Room | Tileset | Theme | Ambient animation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Koga | `TILESET_KOGAS_ROOM` | Shadow Dojo: shoji walls, purple noren over the exit, ninja guardian statues on stone shrines, red lacquered stage, tatami battlefield with the painted crest, poison moats with lantern posts, raked-gravel stone lanterns | poison moats that bubble like the lava in Blaine's gym (the game's own `gfx/tilesets/lava` frames, recoloured purple and always upright; eight independently timed bubble tiles scattered at random, so neighbouring tiles bubble at different moments) |
| Bruno | `TILESET_BRUNOS_ROOM` | Mountain Temple: boulder cliff, twin waterfalls into pools, cracked granite pillars, sandstone dais with stone weights, shimenawa rope over a sealed stone door, circular ringed arena, boulders, training post, fire bowls | falling water (Polished Crystal frame tile), churning splash and spreading ripples at the base, flickering fire bowls |
| Karen | `TILESET_KARENS_ROOM` | Midnight Manor: damask walls, stained-glass windows with bat and cat silhouettes (the moon and the cat in the left window only), grand pointed archway with a five-candle gilt chandelier, violet harlequin dais, starlit dark marble hall with a full-moon medallion and moon-phase ring, gothic columns, gargoyle niches, candelabras, iron portcullis | two bats fly across the left window, then the right window, then back (never in both at once; a 17-second loop), flickering candelabra and chandelier flames, black fire in the niche braziers |

## Art style

The rooms follow the Gen 2 indoor conventions of the original game: every
palette's lightest shade is the GSC warm white (30,28,26) and its darkest is
(7,7,7), the mid tones are the bright, saturated GSC hues (water blue, cave
brown, stone gray, red), textures repeat on the 16x16 grid, and objects are
small outlined sprites with a top-left highlight. Ninja statues, candelabras, the
chandelier and the braziers are hand-placed pixel sprites in the room scripts.

## What was preserved

Map IDs, map sizes, every warp, the trainer positions (Koga, Bruno, Karen
and Lorelei at 5,7; Lance at 5,3), Lance's two approach triggers at 4,5 and
5,5 (row 5 is still only walkable in those two columns), the Oak/Mary
ending paths, scene scripts, event flags and battle scripts. Only the
`changeblock` block IDs for the sealing entrance and the opening exit
changed, because the blocks are new.

## How it works

* `constants/tileset_constants.asm` appends five consecutive tilesets;
  `data/tilesets.asm` and `gfx/tilesets.asm` add their entries. Each part
  (GFX, metatiles, attributes, collision) is its own floating section.
* `LoadSpecialMapPalette` farcalls `LoadLeagueRoomPalette`
  (`engine/tilesets/league_room_palettes.asm`) for those five tilesets.
  Palette 7 stays the text palette.
* Ambience: the five animation tables (`data/tilesets/league_room_anim_tables.asm`)
  hand each step to the ROM0 `LeagueRoomAnimTrampoline`, which runs
  `LeagueRoomAnim` (`engine/tilesets/league_room_anims.asm`, its own bank).
  A descriptor either copies one of a tile's frames into bank-0 VRAM (frame
  chosen from `hVBlankCounter` through a sequence) or cycles a palette in
  hardware palette RAM only (`wBGPals1/2` keep the authored colours, so fades
  and text boxes are unaffected). Descriptors and frames are generated by the
  room scripts. The old unreferenced `UnusedTilesetAnim_*` tables were removed
  from bank $3F.
* `engine/events/mom_phone.asm` (only reached by `farcall`) moved to its own
  section to give bank $3F room for the tables.
* Lance's statues, wall reliefs and floor inlay reuse the game's existing
  Dragonite overworld sprite (`gfx/sprites/dragon.png`), recoloured in gold.
* Entrance doorways: the top tile row over the passage (the lintel) has the
  BG priority attribute bit (`$80`), so the player walks in *under* it before
  the gate seals; the sealed variant clears that bit. Set it in a room script
  with `canvas.prio[ty, tx] = True` (mirrored/copied with the canvas).
  `tools/record_league_rooms.py` records the walk-in (`*_walk_in.gif`).
* Clean outlines: every ellipse (`emask`) passes through `convexify`, which
  re-orders each side's steps so horizontal runs shrink and vertical runs
  grow towards the widest row (a well-drawn pixel curve, no 1-2-1 kinks), and
  trims lone 1-pixel nubs. Rings use `band(shape, t)`, an exact t-pixel band.
  Polygons (`pmask`) are authored with clean 1:1 / 1:2 / 2:1 slopes and are
  not filtered; Karen's arches are made exactly symmetric before tidying.
  Snowflakes use only vertical and 2:1 lines; the small ones are hand-set.
* Long sequences: tile animations step on a 16-bit frame counter
  (`hVBlankCounter` plus `wLeagueAnimCounterHi`, which counts its wraps via
  `wLeagueAnimLastVBlank`), so a sequence may have up to 256 steps at any
  speed. Both WRAM bytes reuse former padding.
* Private animated tiles: an animation entry with `cells=[...]` instead of
  `cell=` gets its own tile that is never shared by picture, so many cells
  can show the same frame yet animate on separate timings (Koga's lava,
  Karen's bat band, whose cells include the window frame).
* Up to 255 tiles: image tiles 0-126 load to bank 0, image tile 127 is
  never loaded (left blank), tiles 128+ load to VRAM bank 1.

## Editing the art

The art is authored as code in `tools/e4_rooms/` (one script per room plus
the shared `e4kit.py`). Each script paints the full room at native
resolution with per-8x8 palettes and per-step collision; the builder
deduplicates tiles (with flips) and blocks and writes the tileset PNG,
palette, metatiles, attributes, collision and `.ablk`.

    python3 tools/e4_rooms/build_all.py

regenerates all five rooms, rewrites the `changeblock` IDs in the map
scripts, and writes previews (with collision overlays) to
`_artifacts/league-rooms-art/`.

## Tests

* `python3 tools/record_league_rooms.py [--rom pokecrystal_debug.gbc]`
  walks each chamber natively: dedicated tileset and palettes, open gate,
  native entry scene sealing the entrance, trainer position and reachability,
  closed exit, opened exit warp to the next room, and ambient animation.
  Screenshots, a contact sheet and animated GIFs go to `_artifacts/league-rooms/`.
* `tools/test_league_progression.py` (the Lorelei entrance check now reads
  the collision of the entrance block instead of a fixed block ID) and
  `make test-streamed-map` cover the rooms too.
