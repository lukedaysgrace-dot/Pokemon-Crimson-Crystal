# Item obtainability — November 18, 2026

All 286 active named item/TM/HM entries have acquisition sources in the current
source. All 70 catalogued held items have player sources. The former list of
eight unplaced items was stale:

| Item | Current source |
| --- | --- |
| Big Root | Hidden in Viridian Forest at (9, 6). |
| Clear Amulet | Burned Tower 1F item ball at (14, 1). |
| Covert Cloak | Team Rocket Base B3F item ball at (3, 12). |
| EXP Relay | Professor Elm after earning six total badges. |
| Light Clay | Kabuto secret item room, Ruins of Alph, at (2, 6); use Flash in the Kabuto chamber. |
| Metronome | Beat all eight Kimono Girls without using healing items, losing or leaving the Dance Theatre. |
| Punching Glove | Mt. Mortar 1F Inside item ball at (17, 31). |
| Safety Goggles | Route 32 item ball at (8, 10). |

This does not mean every item is guaranteed on every save:

- **Metronome is missable.** Leaving or using healing items during the challenge,
  or losing a battle, permanently fails it. Older saves with girls already
  defeated cannot start a fresh challenge.
- **Covert Cloak and Light Clay reuse existing item-ball flags.** Older saves
  that collected the original X Special or Berry cannot collect those replacements.
- **Bicycle and Skateboard are a choice.** Both have sources, but the Bike Shop
  gives only one ride per save.
- **Eon Mail requires compatible infrared Mystery Gift.** It has no ordinary
  single-player acquisition route. The Goldenrod Pokémon Center's Eon Mail
  refund returns an existing copy; it is not a new acquisition source.
- **Park Balls and Safari Balls are activity allowances.** They are supplied for
  the Bug-Catching Contest and Safari Game, respectively.
- **Ability Capsule is conditional on abilities being enabled** for the save.
  Some other sources also depend on progress, weekdays or a random held-item roll.

Custap Berry, Salac Berry, Black Sludge, BrightPowder, Red Card, Heavy-Duty
Boots, Blunder Policy and Eject Button were intentionally removed. Their saved
IDs stay reserved and old saved copies normalize to no item. Retired duplicate
berries are also excluded from the active catalog.

Wild Ditto holds Metal Powder in its common 20% slot or Quick Powder in its rare
5% slot. White Herb, Power Herb and Throat Spray cost ₽1,000 each at the
grandmother's Mahogany Mart shop after clearing the Rocket Hideout.
