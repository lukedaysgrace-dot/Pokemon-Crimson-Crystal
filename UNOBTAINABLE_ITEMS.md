# Unplaced items (no player source yet)

| LIGHT_CLAY | Light Clay |

Custap Berry, Salac Berry, Black Sludge, BrightPowder, Red Card, Heavy-Duty
Boots, Blunder Policy and Eject Button were removed at the user's request.
Their saved IDs are reserved and old saved copies normalize to no item.

Wild Ditto is the only source of Metal Powder (common 20% slot) and Quick
Powder (rare 5% slot). Catch Ditto or take its held item with Thief/Trick.

Eon Mail is available only through a compatible Mystery Gift infrared session.
Park Balls and Safari Balls are supplied for their respective activities.

White Herb, Power Herb and Throat Spray are sold by the grandmother in
Mahogany Mart after the Rocket Hideout is cleared: ₽1,000 each, unlimited stock,
whenever she is present. Her original Crystal supplies are retained; the eleven
former battle items moved to the Battle Tower's selectable win prizes.

To place an unplaced item, add a map pickup/gift, wild held-item assignment or
active mart inventory, then regenerate the item catalog and ITEM_LOCATIONS.md.
