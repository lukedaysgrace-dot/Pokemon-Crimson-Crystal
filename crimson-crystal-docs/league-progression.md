# Sixteen-badge campaign

The main challenge runs through Johto's eight gyms, Kanto's eight gyms, and
then Victory Road and the Elite Four.

- The southern officer in the Reception Gate still requires eight badges.
  Turn east there to reach Route 22 and mainland Kanto. The two northern
  officers require all sixteen badges to enter Victory Road.
- Kanto is first reached overland. Elm rings immediately on leaving Surge's
  gym after earning the Thunder Badge, with the regular phone handler as a
  fallback. He asks the player to return to his lab for a surprise gift and
  explains that he has paid the S.S. Aqua crew for their return voyage.
  This grants one ticket-free trip from Vermilion to Olivine on any weekday.
  Boarding consumes that fare; phone reminders never grant another one.
- Elm gives the permanent S.S. Ticket and Master Ball at the lab after
  Surge. The Rising Badge no longer triggers the Master Ball or its call.
  Olivine's exit remains open after the prepaid return, including dock saves;
  ordinary eastbound voyages require the permanent ticket. The first
  sailing from either port runs the granddaughter quest; later sailings
  keep their weekday schedule. Full pockets preserve any unclaimed gift.
- The Pokégear's full Kanto map and the Pokédex's Kanto area view become
  available with all eight Johto badges.
- Defeating Janine clears the Route 19 shoreline south of Fuchsia.
  Visiting Route 20 no longer clears the rocks.
- The League entrance also checks for sixteen badges, covering players
  who can already Fly to Indigo Plateau in older saves.
- Oak directs sixteen-badge trainers to the League. Mt. Silver requires
  all sixteen badges, a first League victory, the Johto rematch chain through
  Clair, and Crystal's Cerulean Cape battle. Crystal's existing Earth Badge
  call and cape event remain intact. Falkner's rematch starts after the first
  League victory, rather than after Red; every later leader requires the
  preceding rematch. Lance and Oak point the Champion to Falkner.
- Route 23's first-championship blockade still requires the Johto gym
  rematch chain through Clair before the Elite Four rematch.
- The Route 26/27 phone trainers use their initial teams before Surge,
  level-58 rematch aces after Surge, and level-74 aces after the League.
  Restoring Kanto's power no longer selects their final tier early.
- The other Johto phone trainers keep their existing unlocks. Their
  Elite Four tier now has a level-74 ace, and the final tier (Elite Four
  plus Kanto's power) has a level-77 ace, matching the first post-League
  cap rather than the old Johto-only League.

Clair, Elm, the Blackthorn gym guide, Oak, the Route 26/27 trainers and
the Route 27 house explain the new order. Silver's Victory Road dialogue
now fits a journey through Kanto before the League. Ship dialogue does not
assume an exact current badge count. Statements about eight gyms in each
individual region remain correct.

## Levels

Hard Mode caps by total badges, before the Hall of Fame:

| Badges | Cap |
| --- | --- |
| 0–7 | 10, 16, 20, 25, 30, 34, 38, 45 |
| 8–15 | 50, 54, 58, 62, 62, 62, 66, 69 |
| 16 | 73 |

After the Hall of Fame with sixteen badges, caps follow the next Johto
rematch: Falkner 77, Bugsy 78, Whitney 79, Morty 80, Chuck 81, Jasmine 82,
Pryce 83 and Clair 84. Only the consecutive completed rematches advance
this cap; stray later rematch flags do not skip an unfinished leader.
After Clair, Mt. Silver has a cap of 90; defeating Red lifts it to 100.
An old Hall of Fame flag with fewer badges does not skip a cap entry.
Normal Mode remains uncapped. Existing event IDs and save layouts stay intact.

Cal in Viridian's Trainer House picks his team from the player's
highest-level party member when the battle starts:

| Player's highest level | Cal's team |
| --- | --- |
| Below 60 | Meganium, Feraligatr, Typhlosion at 50 |
| 60–69 | The same three plus Charizard, all at 60 |
| 70–79 | The same four plus Venusaur, all at 70 |
| 80+ | The same five plus Blastoise, all at 80 |

Each uses its strongest ability: Mega Sol, Strong Jaw, Adaptability, Drought,
Thick Fat and Mega Launcher.

Erika, Janine and Sabrina share the same ace and Hard Mode cap so all six
orders work. The three Kanto rod groups start at levels 40–49, then add
0/4/8/12/12/12/16/19/23 levels for 0–8 Kanto badges. The middle three
badges therefore share a fishing tier too. Mt. Silver fishing is 70–85.
Species, moves, held items and abilities are preserved in the trainer parties;
ordinary route opponents, first ship passengers and rival fights are relevelled
along with the bosses. The initial Kanto Super Rod level-75 catches no longer
undermine the early Kanto curve. Route 1 and Pallet Town stay sealed behind
Viridian's battling trainers until Janine, so they share the southern-coast
tier: trainer aces 63–64 and wild levels 60–64.

These choices align opponent levels and caps with travel order. The EXP audit
is a screening estimate, not a full campaign playthrough or a claim that every
six-Pokémon team reaches every cap without extra training.

The Rising Badge still grants full obedience to traded Pokémon. Keeping
that reward makes the Kanto journey usable with the existing gym levels.
Johto fishing still scales using only Johto badges; Kanto fishing follows the
new Kanto tiers, and other special groups retain their authored levels.
The wild encounter badge check
is a five-Johto-badge Safari species gate, not a League unlock. Type boosts
already inspect both regions' badge bytes and need no change.

## Verification

Run from the repository root with the built ROM and matching symbols:

```sh
make
python3 tools/test_league_progression.py
python3 tools/test_overworld_state.py
python3 tools/audit_trainers.py
python3 tools/audit_exp_economy.py
```

The progression test executes the compiled scripts and routines using private
emulator state, without loading or modifying player saves. It checks access,
ticket eligibility and retry behavior, both Elm gifts, port callbacks, Oak's
milestones, the complete rematch chain, Janine's shoreline, Clair's advice,
the rematch blockade, map visibility, native fishing routes, and compiled
boss and encounter levels. The overworld test checks both difficulties and
every badge count with and without the Hall of Fame flag, plus all 256
combinations of Johto rematch flags and Red's final cap unlock.
