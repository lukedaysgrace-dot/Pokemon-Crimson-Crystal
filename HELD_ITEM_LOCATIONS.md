# Crimson Crystal — held-item locations

Audited against the current source on November 18, 2026. All 70 catalogued held items have an acquisition route. This is a source audit, not a complete playthrough.

Most gifts and item balls are one-time. Catch wild Pokémon or use Thief with an empty-handed Pokémon; Trick also takes held items. Common wild held slots are 20%, rare slots 5%. Take Air Balloon before damaging the holder. Battle Tower prizes require a completed seven-win challenge; choose one item each time.

**Metronome is missable:** beat all eight Kimono Girls without using healing items, losing or leaving the theatre. Held berries and healing moves are allowed. Old saves with girls already defeated cannot start a fresh challenge. Light Clay and Covert Cloak reuse old item-ball flags, so saves that already collected those balls cannot collect the replacements.
