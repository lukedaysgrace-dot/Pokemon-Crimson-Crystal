ACCURACY IS THE FOUNDATION
What the results mean
One battle engine
Battles execute the compiled debug ROM through the existing PyBoy harness. Damage, abilities, items, type interactions and weight-based move power use your actual game code.
Low Kick and Grass Knot use the target’s coded weight. Heavy Slam and Heat Crash use the coded user-to-target weight ratio. Weights are extracted from the Pokédex records used by the engine.
Strength has conditions
Score is (wins + half of draws) / resolved battles. Errors, failed assertions and turn limits stay visible and are excluded. 1v1 and team battles are separate formats.
Species scores average the builds you tested. They do not establish the globally strongest build. Net HP removed and surviving HP are diagnostics, not independent offensive or defensive tier lists.
Reproducible experiments
Each run records ROM, symbol, source and data hashes, builds, policies and seeds. Side-swapped matchups share a seed. Cross-pool comparisons give every candidate the same opponent seeds.
Runs checkpoint after each battle. Pause takes effect at battle boundaries. Resume replays only uncommitted work and rejects changed game or laboratory versions.
Behavior versus correctness
Executing the ROM reproduces its bugs too. Scenario assertions and the existing semantic harness suites check intended behavior separately. A successful battle does not certify every mechanic.
Tactical and aggressive policies use approximate scoring to choose actions. Native rollout evaluates choices by executing two actual ROM turns using a separate sample. Outcomes always come from the ROM.
