Add Route 41 FINIZEN Sunday encounter

FINIZEN appears at 26,46 on Sundays only. Talking to it makes it cry and
bolt to a new patch of open water twice; the third interaction corners it
and starts a level 30 wild battle. The chase resets on map entry, so all
three have to happen in one visit.

Losing the battle sets EVENT_ROUTE_41_FINIZEN_FOUGHT, which re-arms the
encounter for the following Sunday without the chase. Catching it sets
EVENT_ROUTE_41_CAUGHT_FINIZEN and retires it for good.

A wild battle reports WIN for a knockout as well as a catch, so generalize
the Celebi-only result bit into BATTLERESULT_CAUGHT, set by PokeBallEffect
on any successful catch and cleared per battle by ClearBattleRAM, and add a
CheckCaughtWildMon special to read it. The CheckCaughtMew approach does not
work here: FINIZEN is already in Route 41's water encounters at level 20, so
a dex-flag check would retire the event mon for anyone who had caught one.

SPRITE_FINIZEN takes ENTEI's slot in CianwoodGroupSprites so its 12 tiles
land in VRAM bank 1. It spins nonstop while on screen, which per the group's
existing notes is the worst thing to leave in bank 0's font-shared step-frame
region; ENTEI's Cianwood City cameo is a short scripted beat and moves down
instead. Bank 0 goes from 36/128 to 48/128.

wFinizenPosition comes out of the existing padding after wFarfetchdPosition
(ds 13 -> ds 12), so nothing after it shifts and existing saves still load.
