# Elite AI Tier (AI_ELITE) — 2026-08-14

Boss trainers now play with full knowledge of the player's ACTIVE mon —
real ability, held item, typing — while remaining strictly blind to the
player's pending action each turn. Predictions stay rewarded: the AI
commits its move against the mon that was out when the player chose, so
switching a Levitate mon into a telegraphed Earthquake always works.

## Design rules

1. **Full static knowledge, no turn-reading.** The AI may read the
   player's ability (`GetOppIgnorableAbility`), item (`wBattleMonItem`),
   typing, weather, and Choice-lock state (`wPlayerChoiceLockedMove` —
   established by a move already used). It must NEVER read
   `wBattlePlayerAction`, `wCurPlayerMove`, `wPlayerIsSwitching`,
   `wCurBattleMon`/`wCurPartyMon`, or anything else describing the
   player's current-turn selection. Verified by audit.
2. **Bench blindness.** No elite logic evaluates the player's party,
   only the active mon.

## The flag

- `AI_ELITE` = bit 11 of the AI flags word
  (`constants/trainer_data_constants.asm`), plus `AI_ELITE_F EQU 11`.
- Granted in `data/trainers/attributes.asm` to 41 classes: all Johto +
  Kanto gym leaders, Rival1/Rival2, Will/Koga/Bruno/Karen/Champion,
  Agatha/Lorelei, Executivem/Executivef, Proton/Petrel/Ariana/Archer/
  PetrelDirector, Red/Red2, Blue/Blue Cloak, Green, Mysticalman,
  Pokemon Prof, Cal, Crystal/Crystal2. Cooltrainers and grunts stay on
  plain AI_SMART.
- **Battle Tower**: tower battles resolve AI attributes to class 1
  (Falkner) — Falkner has AI_ELITE, so every tower trainer is elite.
- The full 16-bit flags word is cached at battle start in the
  previously-dead `wEnemyTrainerAIFlags` (c653/c654) by
  `GetTrainerAttributes` (with the tower override), so any bank tests
  elite with `ld a, [wEnemyTrainerAIFlags + 1] / bit AI_ELITE_F - 8, a`.
  `InitEnemyWildmon` clears the high byte.

## New behaviors

### `AI_Elite` scoring layer (`engine/battle/ai/scoring.asm`, slot 11)
- Dismisses (+30) status moves the player's ability/typing makes fail:
  sleep vs Insomnia/Vital Spirit; paralysis vs Limber, Electric-types,
  or type-immune targets (T-Wave vs Ground); Toxic/poison vs Immunity/
  Pastel Veil/Poison/Steel (unless our Corrosion, effective-ability
  checked); Will-O-Wisp vs Fire-types/Water Veil/Thermal Exchange/Flash
  Fire; confusion/Swagger vs Own Tempo; Attract vs Oblivious; Leech
  Seed vs Grass; any Grass status move vs Sap Sipper; Leaf Guard in sun.
- Dismisses stat-drops vs Clear Body/White Smoke/Mirror Armor/Contrary
  and vs Defiant/Competitive (free boosts); Hyper Cutter/Big Pecks/
  Keen Eye/Mind's Eye per-stat.
- OHKO moves: dismissed vs Sturdy, discouraged vs full-HP Focus Sash.
  (Note: Guillotine has 0 listed power in `data/moves/moves.asm` while
  Horn Drill/Fissure have 1; the layer routes on EFFECT_OHKO before the
  power test to cover it.)
- Won't Explode into a full-HP Sash/Sturdy survivor.
- **Choice-lock exploitation**: if the player is locked into a damaging
  move that cannot touch us (type immunity or our nullifying ability),
  the turn is treated as free — setup moves and Substitute get -3.

### Speed (`AICompareSpeed`)
Elite trainers route through `CompareSpeedsWithAbilities` (the real
move-order comparison): Choice Scarf, Quick Feet, weather speed
abilities, Unburden, Klutz, paralysis, Trick Room. All ~18 AI_Smart
speed-gated handlers (Explosion timing, priority scoring, etc.) get
this for free. Non-elite keeps the raw-stat compare.

### KO estimation (`AI_SmartDamageKO`)
Elite trainers no longer treat a "guaranteed KO" as guaranteed when the
player sits at full HP with a Focus Sash or Sturdy
(`AIElitePlayerDeniesKO`).

### Switch AI (`engine/battle/ai/switch.asm`)
- **Own Choice lock**: `CheckEnemyMoveEffectiveness` and
  `.CheckEnemyMoveMatchups` grade ONLY the locked move
  (`AIEliteSkipUnlessOwnLock`). A mon locked into an immune/useless
  move now routes into the maximum-urgency switch branch instead of
  sitting there clicking a blanked move.
- **Player's Choice lock**: `CheckPlayerMoveTypeMatchups` weighs only
  the player's locked move when deciding whether to stay or switch
  (`AIEliteSkipUnlessPlayerLock`).
- **Post-KO replacement** (`AIPickPostKOSwitchIn`, farcalled from
  `EnemyPartyMonEntrance`): replaces the legacy first-index scan for
  elite trainers. Preference order: healthy mon that resists the player
  AND has a super-effective move → healthy mon with SE (or neutral)
  coverage → healthy mon that resists the player → legacy fallback.
  Sets enemy turn first (matchup helpers assume it); writes
  `wEnemySwitchMonIndex` (1-based, 0 = legacy path).

## Plumbing notes

- All new scoring/switch code lives in the "Enemy Trainers" bank
  (same-bank `call`s between scoring.asm and switch.asm are legal;
  bank at $270c/$4000 after the change). Battle Core is at $3fc9/$4000
  — nearly full; the hook additions cost ~10 bytes.
- `farcall` clobbers a and hl at the call site; bc/de and callee flags
  survive (ReturnFarCall). Every new farcall site push/pops what it
  needs.
- `AIElitePlayerDeniesKO` and the layer assume `hBattleTurn` = enemy;
  all call sites guarantee it.
- Adversarially reviewed: turn-direction bug in the post-KO picker and
  the Guillotine power-0 routing were caught and fixed; fairness audit
  (no pending-action reads) is CLEAN.

## Known limits / future ideas

- Item-bag AI (potions/X-items) unchanged — still HP-threshold based.
- No incoming-damage prediction for heal timing.
- `FindEnemyMonsWithASuperEffectiveMove` inherits the vanilla quirk of
  not resetting `e` per bench mon (neutral-coverage set can over-fill).
- Sound-move statuses (Sing) vs Soundproof not modeled (list lives in
  the abilities bank).
- Trick/Knock Off item-value reasoning unchanged.
